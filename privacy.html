DynamicLand Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

DynamicLand respects your privacy.

Google Calendar

When you connect Google Calendar, DynamicLand accesses calendar information required to display and manage your calendar events.

Depending on the features you use, DynamicLand may read, create, edit, and delete calendar events on your behalf.

Google Calendar authentication credentials are stored securely on your device. DynamicLand does not sell your Google Calendar data.

Disconnecting Google Calendar

You can disconnect your Google Calendar account from DynamicLand at any time through the application's Calendar settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support information provided by DynamicLand.
