# Design and interaction contract

Rose-gray canvas (#E6DBE1), muted mauve surfaces (#DBCED6), and pale rose cards (#EEE5EB); near-black plum headings, muted plum-gray body copy, genuine purple app icon. Official #8810F8 → #D986FF gradient restricted to short decorative brand accents; readable headline accent uses solid dark purple. 1192px continuous content alignment; wide gallery capped at 1456px. 24px desktop / 16px compact gaps; 20–28px fluid outer gutters; 22px media radii. System sans; hero 40–62px, sections 30–42px, supporting prose 17–18px. Standard background section insets use 72–112px, related chapter transitions use 80–144px total, and independent same-background chapter boundaries use a fluid 96–224px with a short-viewport limit. One dark music chapter, curated 2:1:1 utility grid, shared customization stage.

Reference evidence: CleanShot for hierarchy/alignment; LaunchMe for large scrolling stages and paired compositions; Apple for preview timing/interruption. Live pages and gallery controls inspected. No proprietary implementation/assets copied. The initial build used live browser captures where standalone screenshots were unavailable. The later timed-carousel correction also uses the supplied CleanShot Apple control recording and sections 5–6 of the Apple analysis. See `carousel-correction.md`.

Pricing now uses a compact, equal-width Free/Pro pair under “Start free. Go further.” The Free card uses pale rose; the Pro card uses the owner-provided purple floral/cloud illustration with a contrast gradient and a rose-gray action. Three short benefit rows replace the long lists, actions align on desktop, and the cards stack below 760px. Subscription terms and the current App Store pricing destination remain explicit. The section is 844px tall at 1440px and 1231px at 390px, about 150px and 266px shorter respectively than the previous version. Existing chapter spacing is preserved. Captures and layout measurements are in `artifacts/pricing/`. The production build, scoped ESLint, and eight existing Chromium/WebKit checks passed (two existing WebKit skips), including accessibility, no-JavaScript content, destinations, viewport bounds, and chapter rhythm.

The revised motion pass adapts the app's source-to-destination folder geometry, shell/content handoff, and lyric illumination. Generic card entrances from the first pass were removed. The source evidence, native-to-web decisions, and interruption model are documented in [Native motion study](native-motion-study.md). Source inspection did not include launching or profiling the native app. Native spring response values and finite web animation durations describe different quantities; the web values are deliberately tuned for their website surfaces.

Native page scroll, no pinned empty scene and no WebGL. Native requestAnimationFrame coordinates the gallery clock and scroll travel; WAAPI handles finite caption/section animations. Motion Mini coordinates the one-shot gallery-control entrance described in [Gallery entrance](gallery-entrance.md). Observers and listeners clean up on unmount. Intentional blank slots contain only small neutral Photo/Video identifiers, hidden from accessibility and snippets. Media playback/zoom controls require real assets. The highlights slideshow rotation control is explicitly timed by slide dwell and works with empty slots.

Pro badges share the native app's actual wordmark: bold rounded white PRO lettering over its blue/purple/lilac/peach mesh, with continuous rounded corners and a soft blue shadow. A single native-rendered image is used by the reusable `ProBadge` component for section labels, gallery labels, customization options, and the pricing plan. The mark has an accessible text alternative, scales with text size, and reserves its layout space before loading. It stays static in both motion modes. Inline prose retains normal text.

Compact navigation expands from the menu circle, keeping its final top and right edges aligned with that circle. It has a 320px maximum width, 24px corners, and a lightly frosted 14px backdrop blur. The circle is consumed by the panel; there is no X. Outside presses dismiss it, including presses elsewhere in the header. Its closed trigger uses the same glass material inside a 44px circle. The owner's later video supplies the circle-to-panel opening, delayed optical text reveal, and returning droplet closure; see [Mobile glass menu](mobile-menu-morph.md) for measured geometry and interruption behavior. It uses translucent purple glass over the hero and pale rose glass on the page, with an opaque fallback for reduced transparency or unsupported filters. The open header removes its own backdrop filter so the expanding panel can sample the page. Links have generous touch targets and quiet directional arrows; short viewports scroll inside the panel.
