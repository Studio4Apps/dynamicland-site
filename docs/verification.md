### Visual and motion inspection

Screenshots were opened and visually inspected, including readable chapter crops and all six viewport families: desktop, mobile, tablet, short landscape, near-square and ultrawide. Review covered headings/line breaks, blank-stage ratios, CTA placement, consistent alignment, the 2:1:1 grid and tablet/mobile rearrangement, legal/support readability and original social card. Fixed headers appearing in the middle of some element-capture images are capture artifacts; full-page crops and viewport captures were used to inspect actual composition.

The final rhythm pass additionally measured every top-level homepage boundary, inspected matching full-page desktop/tablet/mobile captures, and reviewed normal-size crops around all changed transitions. Independent white-on-white chapters now use 96.34px mobile, 158.72px tablet, and 224px desktop settled gaps; related Pricing → FAQ uses 80px mobile/tablet and 144px desktop. Background-changing chapters retained their already clear internal insets.

The 9.12-second recording covers gallery movement/reversal, preview changes and Home/music blur moments. It was reviewed through chronological frames across its full duration and denser 12fps transition strips. Text remains sharp, frames settle, and selection/labels follow navigation. Local rAF profiling during a 1.2-second gallery movement: 74 sampled frames, median 16.7ms, p95/max 16.8ms, no observed long tasks in the recorded sequence. This is a small unthrottled lab sample, not a universal frame-rate guarantee.

A separate 16.4-second continuous whole-page scroll and rapid reverse recording was reviewed through one-second frames. Its 132 samples observed the declared 14px maximum reveal translation and 0.5 minimum entrance opacity; both normal and reduced-motion passes settled with no running reveal animations, horizontal overflow, or console/page errors.

Fixes from QA include neutral-label contrast, restoring a usable mobile summary focus starting point, media-source validation/failure coverage, cancellation/selection handling, the timed carousel correction, and a whole-homepage rhythm hierarchy for independent, background-changing, and related chapters. The CSP test was corrected to inject into HTTP markup; DevTools evaluation is privileged and is not a valid XSS simulation. Menu tests were corrected to use actual platform keyboard navigation and click outside the open overlay. No protection was relaxed to make tests pass.
