## Revised direction: surface continuity, not card reveals

The owner rejected the initial pass as generic card animation. The current implementation removes ordinary card, pricing-card, heading, and hero-frame entrances. Only the two deliberate optical media entrances remain. Useful disclosure/selector interruption fixes are retained.

Further targeted source inspection covered `NotchMorphEngine.swift` (including the timer's shell-before-content sequence), `DICompactExpandedHandoff.swift` (shared progress and overlapping appearances), `HomeTab/FolderTransition.swift` (source-to-destination geometry and individually carried icons), `Launchpad/LaunchpadFolderMorph.swift` (anchored expansion with delayed labels), `LyricsV2/LyricsMotion.swift` (travelling text illumination), and `VoiceMemo/MemosToWaveformMorph.swift` (source glyphs owning a transformation rather than a disconnected crossfade).

Those behaviors have no equivalent surface here. The previously corrected highlights clock, dwell, and travel timings are preserved.

## Web tuning and resilience

- Headings move only 8px. Supporting cards travel 18px on desktop / 10px on compact screens; panels start at 0.985 scale. Ordinary entrances settle over 520ms; the two optical media entrances over 680ms. Related desktop cards stagger by 55ms, capped at 110ms; compact screens have no stagger.
- Reading content remains fully opaque during section/menu entrances. This also fixes the transient contrast failure found by axe when the hero placeholder label faded with its frame. Opacity transitions are reserved for actual content handoffs and optical media wrappers.
- Initial HTML/CSS is visible. Native radio inputs and `<details>` provide usable controls with JavaScript disabled, missing APIs, or a thrown animation exception. Hero copy never waits for an entrance.
- Motion scopes cancel on resize, hidden document, unmount, and a live reduced-motion preference. Disclosures normalize to their latest requested open state. Keyboard focus cancels section entrances to keep focused controls stationary.
- Reduced motion uses immediate state changes and removes travel, blur, scale feedback, and smooth scrolling. Finished entrances do not replay on return scroll. Native page scrolling remains intact.
- Disclosure clipping applies only to the answer, leaving the summary's focus outline intact. Closing answer content is inert until its native disclosure state is settled.
