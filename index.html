DynamicLand

DynamicLand is a macOS workspace application that brings together customizable widgets, calendar views, media controls, clipboard and file tools, and an optional AI Assistant.

Google Calendar Integration

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. When you connect an account, DynamicLand uses your Google account identifier and email address to identify the connection, and reads your calendar list so you can choose which calendars to display.

DynamicLand accesses event information to display your schedule, show event details and upcoming-event alerts, and let you create, edit, and delete events where your account has permission. Changes made in DynamicLand are synchronized with Google Calendar.

You can manage or disconnect your Google Calendar connection in DynamicLand's Calendar settings. Disconnecting the account does not delete your events from Google Calendar.
